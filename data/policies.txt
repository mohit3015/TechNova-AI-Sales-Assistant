# TechNova Store - Official Company Policies

## 1. Warranty and Service Policy
- All TechNova laptops come with a standard 1-Year or 2-Year Official Manufacturer Warranty (extendable up to 3 years with TechNova Care+).
- Free On-Site doorstep service is available in 150+ cities across India.
- Warranty covers manufacturing defects, hardware failure, motherboard issues, and screen defects not caused by accidental damage.
- Battery and charger are covered under a 1-year warranty period.
- Physical damage, liquid spills, and unauthorized tampering are excluded from standard warranty coverage unless TechNova Accidental Damage Protection (ADP) is purchased.

## 2. Return, Replacement, and Refund Policy
- TechNova provides a 7-Day Hassle-Free Replacement Policy from the date of delivery.
- If a product is received in a damaged condition, has dead pixels, or exhibits hardware defects, a replacement unit is dispatched within 48 hours of inspection.
- If a customer wishes to return an unopened product, a full refund is initiated within 3 to 5 business days to the original payment source.
- Open-box returns for change-of-mind are subject to a nominal 5% restocking fee if all accessories and packaging are intact.

## 3. Shipping and Delivery Policy
- Free Standard Delivery on all laptop orders across all Indian pin codes.
- Tier-1 Metro Cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata): Delivery in 24 to 48 hours.
- Other Cities and Tier-2/Tier-3 locations: Delivery within 3 to 5 business days.
- All shipments are fully insured against transit damage and theft. Real-time SMS and WhatsApp tracking are provided immediately after dispatch.

## 4. Payment Methods and No-Cost EMI Options
- Accepted payment methods: Credit Cards, Debit Cards, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (available on orders up to ₹50,000).
- No-Cost EMI is available on HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, SBI, Axis Bank, and Kotak Mahindra Bank credit cards for tenures of 3, 6, 9, and 12 months.
- Cardless EMI is available via Bajaj Finserv, ZestMoney, and Snapmint for eligible pre-approved customers.

## 5. Student and Educator Discounts
- Verified college students and teachers receive an additional 5% instant discount (up to ₹4,000) on all TechNova ProBook, CodeMaster, and AI Studio models.
- Verification requires submitting a valid college student ID card or educational email (.edu / .ac.in domain).
- Free TechNova developer backpack and wireless mouse included with every laptop purchase for verified students.

## 6. Trade-In and Laptop Exchange Program
- Customers can exchange their old working laptops and get up to ₹25,000 exchange bonus based on configuration and condition.
- Free doorstep evaluation and instant discount deduction at checkout.
